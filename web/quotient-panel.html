The quotient

Four quantities in one formula, reached by four different routes: a rapidly convergent series, an arithmetic-geometric mean, a local analysis at each bad prime, and a greatest common divisor of point counts. Two of them start from the same coefficients and arrive somewhere entirely different. Assemble all four and the result lands on an integer. Below is that assembly, with what is exact, what is certified and what is merely consistent kept apart.
